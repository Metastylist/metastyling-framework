## System Layer

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Identity Field">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-001-A">
  <link itemprop="inDefinedTermSet" href="#glossary">

### Identity Field

Dynamic space of possible identity configurations through which a system moves over time. Not a fixed structure but navigable terrain containing all accessible states at any given moment.

**See also:** [Meta-Person](#meta-person), [State](#state-identity-state--xt)

**Full details:** [What is Metastyling](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/01-what-is-metastyling.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="State (Identity)">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-001-B">

### State (Identity State) — x(t)

Current identity configuration as a weighted combination of active Faces. Represents where you are in the identity field at this moment.

**See also:** [Face](#face), [Face Weight](#face-weight--wkt)

**Full details:** [Master Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/master_equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Face">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-003">

### Face

Stable attractor in the identity field—a recurring configuration that the system naturally settles into. Not a personality type (dynamic, not fixed) or a role/mask (genuine configuration). Most individuals have 5-12 distinct Faces.

**See also:** [Attractor](#attractor), [DMES Signature](#dmes-signature), [Range](#range)

**Full details:** [Faces & Attractors](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/03-faces-attractors.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Face Weight">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-003-A">

### Face Weight — w_k(t)

Relative activation level of a specific Face at time t. All Face weights sum to 1, showing how your current identity is distributed across your Face repertoire.

**See also:** [Face](#face), [State](#state-identity-state--xt)

**Full details:** [Master Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/master_equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Meta-Person">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-001-C">

### Meta-Person

The field of all identity states accessible to a system at a given moment—your possibility space. Not a single fixed self but a distribution over possible configurations, bounded by field topology and navigable through awareness development.

**See also:** [Identity Field](#identity-field), [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa)

**Full details:** [What is Metastyling](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/01-what-is-metastyling.md)

</div>

## DMES Layer

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="DMES">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-002">

### DMES

Four-vector model describing identity configuration: Direction (WHY), Meaning (WHERE), Expression (WHAT), State (HOW). An entangled system where shifting one vector affects all others.

**See also:** [Direction](#direction-d), [Meaning](#meaning-m), [Expression](#expression-e), [State](#state-s)

**Full details:** [DMES Vectors](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/02-dmes-vectors.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="DMES Signature">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-002-E">

### DMES Signature

Characteristic DMES-vector configuration that defines a specific Face. Each Face has a unique signature—a stable pattern across all four dimensions.

**See also:** [Face](#face), [DMES](#dmes)

**Full details:** [Faces & Attractors](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/03-faces-attractors.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Direction">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-002-A">

### Direction (D)

Motivational WHY vector—the clarity and autonomy of your goals. Ranges from reactive (driven by external validation) to autonomous (internally generated).

**See also:** [DMES](#dmes), [State](#state-s)

**Full details:** [Direction Vector](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/02-direction-vector.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Meaning">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-002-B">

### Meaning (M)

Interpretive WHERE vector—how you make sense of experience. Ranges from threat-locked (single rigid interpretation) to multi-lens (flexible perspective-taking).

**See also:** [DMES](#dmes), [Threat-Lock](#threat-lock)

**Full details:** [Meaning Vector](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/02-meaning-vector.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Expression">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-002-C">

### Expression (E)

Communicative WHAT vector—alignment between internal state and external signal. Ranges from collapsed/over-controlled to fully congruent (no performance gap).

**See also:** [DMES](#dmes), [Coherence](#coherence)

**Full details:** [Expression Vector](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/02-expression-vector.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="State (S Vector)">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-002-D">

### State (S)

Operational HOW vector—nervous system regulation level. Ranges from dysregulated (survival mode) to fully regulated (resilient capacity). When State drops below threshold, all other vectors collapse.

**See also:** [DMES](#dmes), [Direction](#direction-d), [Meaning](#meaning-m)

**Full details:** [State Vector](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/02-state-vector.md)

</div>

## Awareness Layer

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Levels of Awareness">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-004">

### Levels of Awareness (LoA)

Observational depth—how clearly you see identity dynamics while they're happening. LoA 0 (immersion), LoA 1 (recognition), LoA 2 (selection), LoA 3 (architecture).

**See also:** [Depth](#depth-authenticity-component), [Modulation](#modulation)

**Full details:** [Levels of Awareness](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/04-levels-of-awareness.md)

**Examples:** [Kendall Roy (LoA 0)](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/case-studies/kendall-roy-analysis.md), [Oprah Winfrey (LoA 2→3)](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/case-studies/oprah-winfrey-transition.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="LoA 0">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-004-A">

### LoA 0: Immersion

Complete identification with current Face—no observational distance. Experience feels like reality itself, not a configuration among many.

**See also:** [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa), [Face Collapse](#face-collapse)

**Full details:** [Levels of Awareness](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/04-levels-of-awareness.md)

**Example:** [Kendall Roy Analysis](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/case-studies/kendall-roy-analysis.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="LoA 1">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-004-B">

### LoA 1: Recognition

Pattern awareness in hindsight—can recognize Faces after they've occurred but not during activation. "I was in Critic mode yesterday."

**See also:** [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa), [Signal](#signal)

**Full details:** [Levels of Awareness](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/04-levels-of-awareness.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="LoA 2">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-004-C">

### LoA 2: Selection

Real-time choice—can observe Face activation as it happens and select different configurations. Strategic navigation of identity field.

**See also:** [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa), [Modulation](#modulation)

**Full details:** [Levels of Awareness](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/04-levels-of-awareness.md)

**Further reading:** [Personal Navigation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/applications/personal-navigation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="LoA 3">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-004-D">

### LoA 3: Architecture

Can observe and reshape field structure itself—modify attractor depth, create new Faces, redesign topology. Landscape architecture of identity.

**See also:** [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa), [Attractor Depth](#attractor-depth)

**Full details:** [Levels of Awareness](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/04-levels-of-awareness.md)

**Further reading:** [Part VI: Laboratory Protocols (PDF)](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/main/Metastyling_Part6.pdf)

</div>

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Modulation">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-005">

### Modulation

Intentional adjustment of identity configuration—changing DMES vectors or Face activation through conscious choice. Only possible at LoA 2+.

**See also:** [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa), [Intention](#intention--ut)

**Full details:** [Modulation Mechanisms](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/05-modulation-mechanisms.md)

</div>

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Intention">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-005-A">

### Intention — u(t)

Conscious input to system dynamics—active choice to shift configuration. Emerges from observational depth: higher LoA enables more precise intention.

**See also:** [Modulation](#modulation), [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa)

**Full details:** [Modulation Mechanisms](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/05-modulation-mechanisms.md)

## Dynamics Layer

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Attractor">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D01">

### Attractor

Stable configuration that system tends toward—a "gravity well" in the identity field. The system naturally settles into attractors and resists moving away from them.

**See also:** [Face](#face), [Attractor Depth](#attractor-depth), [Basin](#basin-of-attraction)

**Full details:** [Faces & Attractors](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/03-faces-attractors.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Attractor Depth">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D02">

### Attractor Depth

Strength of attraction—how stable a Face is and how difficult it is to exit. Deep attractors are easy to enter, hard to exit, and stable once activated. Shallow attractors require specific conditions and collapse quickly.

**See also:** [Attractor](#attractor), [Face](#face), [Trajectory Lock](#trajectory-lock)

**Full details:** [Faces & Attractors](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/03-faces-attractors.md)

**Further reading:** [Cost Function](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/cost-function.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Basin of Attraction">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D02-A">

### Basin of Attraction

Region of identity field where system naturally flows toward a specific attractor. Once you're in the basin, you tend to settle into that Face.

**See also:** [Attractor](#attractor), [Face](#face)

**Full details:** [Faces & Attractors](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/03-faces-attractors.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Trajectory">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D03">

### Trajectory

Actual path through identity field over time—the sequence of states from past to present. Baseline trajectory is natural flow without intervention; modulated trajectory includes conscious choices.

**See also:** [State](#state-identity-state--xt), [Intention](#intention--ut)

**Full details:** [Forecast Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/forecast-equation.md)

**Further reading:** [Master Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/master_equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Bifurcation">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D04">

### Bifurcation

Critical instability where multiple possible paths become available—a fork in the road. Only visible at LoA 2+; this is where conscious choice becomes possible.

**See also:** [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa), [Intention](#intention--ut)

**Full details:** [Levels of Awareness](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/04-levels-of-awareness.md)

**Further reading:** [Forecast Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/forecast-equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Stochastic Fluctuation">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D05">

### Stochastic Fluctuation

Internal noise—micro-variations in mood, energy, and cognitive load that prevent perfect predictability. Small fluctuations can cause large divergences near bifurcation points.

**See also:** [Noise](#noise--ξt), [Bifurcation](#bifurcation)

**Full details:** [Master Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/master_equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Resonance">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-005-C">

### Resonance

External pattern amplifying a dormant Face through frequency matching. When an external stimulus perfectly matches an internal configuration, activation happens effortlessly and instantly.

**See also:** [Face](#face), [Modulation](#modulation)

**Full details:** [Modulation Mechanisms](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/05-modulation-mechanisms.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Disruption">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-005-A">

### Disruption

Shock that ejects the system from its current attractor—fast (hours to days) but unpredictable and unstable. Examples include crisis, trauma, or major loss.

**See also:** [Attractor](#attractor), [Modulation](#modulation)

**Full details:** [Modulation Mechanisms](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/05-modulation-mechanisms.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Observation (Modulation)">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-005-B">

### Observation (Modulation Mechanism)

Repeated self-seeing that gradually reshapes the identity landscape. Slow (weeks to months) but directional and stable—changes integrate naturally through practice.

**See also:** [Modulation](#modulation), [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa)

**Full details:** [Modulation Mechanisms](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/05-modulation-mechanisms.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Transition">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D06">

### Transition

Shift between attractors (Face A → Face B). Organic transitions are low-cost and self-reinforcing; imposed transitions are high-cost and require constant maintenance.

**See also:** [Face](#face), [Transition Cost](#transition-cost), [Alignment](#alignment--α)

**Full details:** [Cost Function](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/cost-function.md)

**Further reading:** [Time Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/time-equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Strategic Micro-Shift">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-D07">

### Strategic Micro-Shift

Small, targeted adjustment without losing overall coherence. Lasting change through incremental recalibration rather than dramatic disruption.

**See also:** [Transition](#transition), [Coherence](#coherence)

**Full details:** [What is Metastyling](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/01-what-is-metastyling.md)

</div>

## Cost & Resource Layer

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Transition Cost">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-F002-A">

### Transition Cost

Energy and resources required to move between identity states. Cost depends on distance, alignment with field topology, attractor depth, and observational capacity.

**See also:** [Transition](#transition), [Alignment](#alignment--α), [Resource Capacity](#resource-capacity--rt)

**Full details:** [Cost Function](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/cost-function.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Token">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-E02">

### Token

Comparable unit of transition cost—makes abstract energy requirements plannable and comparable. Individually calibrated; one person's 30 tokens may differ from another's.

**See also:** [Transition Cost](#transition-cost)

**Full details:** [Cost Function](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/cost-function.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Resource Capacity">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-E03">

### Resource Capacity — R(t)

Available energy, attention, emotion, and time at any given moment. Varies with State stability, context support, and observational depth.

**See also:** [State](#state-s), [Burn Rate](#burn-rate), [Transition Cost](#transition-cost)

**Full details:** [Time Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/time-equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Alignment">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-E04">

### Alignment — α

Compatibility between your intended trajectory and natural field topology. Organic goals (α ≈ 0) emerge from the field and feel like flow; imposed goals (α ≫ 1) conflict with the field and require constant force.

**See also:** [Transition Cost](#transition-cost), [Trajectory](#trajectory)

**Full details:** [Cost Function](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/cost-function.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Burn Rate">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-E05">

### Burn Rate

Resource consumption rate over time. Sustainable when consumption equals regeneration; unsustainable when depleting resources faster than replenishing.

**See also:** [Resource Capacity](#resource-capacity--rt)

**Full details:** [Time Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/time-equation.md)

</div>

## Stability & Noise Layer

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Coherence">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-S01">

### Coherence

Internal consistency—when all four DMES vectors align and mutually reinforce each other. Required for stable Face activation and sustainable transitions.

**See also:** [DMES](#dmes), [Expression](#expression-e), [Face](#face)

**Full details:** [DMES Vectors](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/core-concepts/02-dmes-vectors.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Identity Drift">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-S02">

### Identity Drift

Slow, unconscious shift over months or years—typically unintentional baseline trajectory. Distinct from deliberate navigation at LoA 2+.

**See also:** [Trajectory](#trajectory), [Levels of Awareness](#levels-of-awareness-loa)

**Full details:** [Forecast Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/forecast-equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Noise">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-S03">

### Noise — ξ(t)

Random fluctuations unrelated to core patterns—background variation in mood, attention, and energy. Makes it difficult to distinguish meaningful signal from random variation.

**See also:** [Stochastic Fluctuation](#stochastic-fluctuation), [Signal](#signal)

**Full details:** [Master Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/master_equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Signal">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-S04">

### Signal

Meaningful behavioral patterns—repeated Face activations that emerge from longitudinal observation. Requires weeks of data to distinguish from random noise.

**See also:** [Noise](#noise--ξt), [Face](#face)

**Full details:** [Master Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/master_equation.md)

</div>

## Forecasting Layer

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Identity Forecasting">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-F003">

### Identity Forecasting

Probabilistic modeling of future identity states based on historical trajectory, field structure, context, LoA, and planned interventions. Not certainty—confidence degrades over time.

**See also:** [Possible Futures](#possible-futures), [Probable Futures](#probable-futures), [Preferred Futures](#preferred-futures)

**Full details:** [Forecast Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/forecast-equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Possible Futures">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-FC01">

### Possible Futures

All theoretically reachable states—full possibility space bounded by field topology. Not all equally accessible; some require high cost or advanced LoA.

**See also:** [Identity Field](#identity-field), [Identity Forecasting](#identity-forecasting)

**Full details:** [Forecast Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/forecast-equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Probable Futures">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-FC02">

### Probable Futures

Likely outcomes from current trajectory if no intervention occurs—baseline forecast based on historical patterns and field inertia.

**See also:** [Identity Drift](#identity-drift), [Preferred Futures](#preferred-futures)

**Full details:** [Forecast Equation](https://github.com/Metastylist/metastyling-framework/blob/formulas/forecast-equation.md)

</div>

---

<div itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/DefinedTerm">
  <meta itemprop="name" content="Preferred Futures">
  <meta itemprop="termCode" content="META-FC03">

### Preferred Futures

Intentionally selected futures through LoA 2+ navigation. Requires observational depth to see options and may differ from probable futures.
